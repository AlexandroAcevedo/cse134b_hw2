MathML Extra Credit

Einstein's mass-energy equivalence:

E=mc2

MathML was part of web standards for years, but developers could not rely on it because browser support was inconsistent, especially across major browser engines. As native MathML support became more broadly available across modern browsers, authors could use it with much less risk that the mathematical notation would fail or require a fallback.
