Why Carnitas Is One of My Favorite Foods

What I Like About It

Carnitas is one of my favorite Mexican dishes because it has a good combination of textures and flavors. The pork can be crispy on the outside while staying tender and juicy inside. I also like that the meat itself has plenty of flavor without needing a complicated list of toppings.

How I Like to Eat It

My favorite way to eat carnitas is in tacos with warm corn tortillas. I usually prefer simple toppings so the carnitas stays the main part of the meal. Depending on the restaurant, I might add onion, cilantro, salsa, or a little lime. Carnitas can also work well in burritos or other dishes, but tacos are my first choice.
